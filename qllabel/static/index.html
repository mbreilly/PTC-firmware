FIND THE PRINTER'S REAL LIMITS

Prints one test label per length: a frame on the exact edge of the image, the length written inside, a tick every millimetre along the top (long tick every 5). Measure what comes out and compare. Try margin 35 (what Brother's driver uses) and then 0.

LENGTHS mm MARGIN dots CUT

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

  • Is each piece as long as written? If the short ones come out longer, the printer pads up to its own minimum.
  • Does the frame touch the cut on both ends? The gap is the feed margin plus the cutter offset.
  • Are the ticks 1 mm apart? (Checks the 300 dpi assumption along the feed.)
  • Text upside down or mirrored compared to what you expect? Tick "turn 180°" on the design tab.

PRINTER

FULL-WIDTH BLEED

Extra dots a label may use beyond Brother's print window when "use the full width" is ticked. Head end: pins left at that end of the print head (max 6 on 29 mm). Open side: towards the far tape edge; keep a few dots short of the edge, the tape wanders a little and pins off the tape heat the bare roller. 29 mm was measured on 2026-10-28: 6 + 18 dots.
